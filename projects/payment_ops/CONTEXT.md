### ReplayEverythingFromCanonicalEvents (2026-03-12)
**Chosen:** "AppendOnlyReplay" — **Rejected:** "MutablePaymentRows", "AdHocStatusOverrides"
> Dirty payment integrations are easiest to audit when the stored history stays immutable. Replaying canonical events keeps dedupe and reconciliation readable ins…
impacts: `hasSourceId`, `nextSeq`, `replayPayment`, `replayAll`

### ManualReviewInsteadOfSilentFixup (2026-03-12)
**Chosen:** "OpenCases" — **Rejected:** "SilentRepair", "LogOnlyWarnings"
> Backoffice systems should not silently fix suspicious payment histories. Opening explicit manual-review cases keeps the risk visible and auditable.
impacts: `draftsFromState`, `materializeNewCases`, `resolveCase`

### SettlementIsEvidenceNotTruth (2026-03-12)
**Chosen:** "CompareAndEscalate" — **Rejected:** "SettlementWinsAlways", "RealtimeWinsAlways"
> Settlement files are operationally important, but they are not always clean or complete. Treating them as evidence instead of unquestioned truth leaves room for…
impacts: `reconcileProvider`

### AppendOnlyEventsAndSettlements (2026-03-12)
**Chosen:** "AppendOnlyFactsMutableCases" — **Rejected:** "MutableEverything", "EventSourceCasesToo"
> Webhook and settlement ingestion should preserve raw ordering and dedupe boundaries. Review cases mutate over time, so they stay in a rewriteable current-state…
impacts: `loadEvents`, `appendEvents`, `loadSettlements`, `appendSettlements`, `loadCases`, `saveCases`
